# Third-party notices

Urushi's built-in theme catalog adapts all 463 terminal palettes from
[iTerm2-Color-Schemes revision `fc73ce39746540b6d5ec6b91e304785431401d85`](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/tree/fc73ce39746540b6d5ec6b91e304785431401d85/ghostty).
Urushi maps the indexed terminal colors to its own semantic roles and derives
neutral surface colors. The original per-theme acknowledgements are preserved
in [`THEME_CREDITS.md`](THEME_CREDITS.md).
